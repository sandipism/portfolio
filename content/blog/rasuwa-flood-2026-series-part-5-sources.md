## Source-use rules

- Prefer primary technical documents over news summaries.
- Treat preliminary media explanations as provisional.
- When sources conflict, record the conflict instead of selecting one silently.
- Record acquisition date for every satellite image.
- Do not mix different imagery processing levels in quantitative change detection without suitable correction.
